03WHY NOW

A great insurance team is a room full of people you cannot replace.

Everything that makes a claim succeed lives in somebody's head. Which insurer wants what. Which document gets queried. What to write when a diagnosis changes after admission. None of it is written down, and it walks out of the building when they do.

01

Most of the process was never online

Insurance in Indian hospitals still runs on paper, scanning, phone calls and email. There is no system of record for the work itself, only for its output.

02

Nobody notices a gap until it is too late to fix

By the time a query arrives, the patient has gone home. Evidence that could have been created in thirty seconds on the ward now cannot be created at all.

03

The same work is done twice

Once for the patient, once for the claim. Two departments, two sets of paper, one admission, and no shared view between them.

04

The bed stays blocked

Discharge waits on the file. The file waits on people. The next admission waits on the bed.

04THE SHIFT

Rules that keep this out of AI slop territory

Motion equals a state changeNothing floats, bobs, parallaxes or shimmers to fill space. If it moves, that thing genuinely happens in the product.
Real strings onlyIP-40712. Implant sticker. Query raised to theatre. A hospital person should recognise every word on screen.
One thing moves at a timeInside a frame, one change, then rest. Three simultaneous animations is what makes a page feel generated.
No bounce, no spring, no glowcubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1) at 220 to 400ms. Opacity and transform. Nothing pulses for decoration.
Deep green on light, bright green on darkThe accent is one hue with two values. Mint never touches a light background except as a field or a live signal.
Reduced motion is honestprefers-reduced-motion renders the finished frame, not a degraded wobble. Already wired.

Design system

Concept: the ledgerA hospital record sheet set properly. Ruled hairlines, a margin rail carrying the ordinal, tabular figures, generous rag. Not a dev-tools lattice.
TypeSchibsted Grotesk 500 for display at -0.032em tracking. IBM Plex Mono for state, IDs and labels only. Two families, strict roles.
The ordinal lives in the marginYou did not want a label sitting on top of a title. The section number moved into the left rail instead, so the technical layer survives and the headline stands alone.
ColourCanvas #F4F5F1, ink #111511, accent #0F5D36 deep green, #3EEBA3 mint for live state and field blocks, #A96A12 amber for a gap. One dark section, the closer.
