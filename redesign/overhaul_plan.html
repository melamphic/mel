0The position — the clinical Vanta

Decided: lead with the compliance vision, not the scribe. Worldwide. Frameworks are the differentiation, not countries.
Salvia is the compliance platform for clinical care — the “clinical Vanta.” Vanta leads “Trust is everything,” not “we automate paperwork.” We run the same, correct playbook for clinical: connect your data → an always-on agent continuously proves your posture → against any framework, worldwide. The scribe lane (Nabla, Abridge, Augnito, Eka) is a red ocean; “continuous clinical compliance, any framework” is an empty category we get to define. Nobody sells “prove any framework from the clinical record” — anywhere.

The homepage hero — three pillars (the Vanta pattern, clinical)

Connect
Your clinical data points
Voice notes, consent, prescriptions, drug administration, incidents, vitals — and your HMIS / EMR. Salvia turns each into structured, checkable evidence.
The agent
Always-on, self-improving
Reads that evidence and continuously proves your posture — drafts, flags gaps before the audit, maps every note to your standards. You confirm; it never submits unsupervised.
Any framework
Global by design
NABH, JCI, NSQHS, CMS, HEDIS and more — one capture engine maps to all of them. Add a framework, not a rebuild. The framework is the localization; the site never names a country.
The wedge, handled — so leading compliance costs nothing. A doctor doesn’t wake up wanting compliance. So the homepage sells the vision, but the “For Clinicians” page leads with the daily clinical win (hours saved, in your language, before you leave the room). Pilot doctors land there and see themselves. Vision up front, the doctor’s relief one click in.
Hero headline — candidates to pick from:
  • “Compliance that proves itself — from the clinical record.”
  • “Every note, continuously proving your compliance. Any framework.”
  • “The compliance agent for clinical care. Connected, always on, global.”

Sub-headline names the mechanism: “Connect your clinical data; an always-on agent maps every note to your framework and flags the gaps before an audit does.”

1Why the current site feels like a confused mess

One root cause, not a hundred small ones.
The redesign built a gorgeous front end, but left the old back end running underneath it. The nav, Landing and /hospitals sell one story; the footer, pricing engine, /frameworks, /start, the blog and the structured data sell a different one — a per-seat, multi-country, four-vertical SaaS. INDIA_ONLY=true is a view-filter bolted over that machinery, so the seams show on every secondary page. A visitor can’t tell which product is real, or that the actual north star — the agentic-compliance vision — exists at all.

What’s already good — reuse, don’t nuke

  • The grass design system — white canvas, one green #48CD5F, Archivo, hairlines, .g-* primitives. Confirmed: this stays. Clean, human, and a differentiator in a category that all looks the same shade of corporate navy.
  • The /hospitals page — the clearest articulation on the site (“That gap is the product.”). Use its structure as the template.
  • The drawn product UIs — register showcase, form-builder, policy-check rows, PDF SHA-256 card, coverage map. Screenshot-fidelity, product-true. More distinctive than any stock photo. High reuse.
  • The sourced stats — real, cited negligence/records data. Keep the substance; rebalance where it leads.
  • The multi-framework machinery + SSG/SEO plumbing — the mechanism is solid. We keep it worldwide (frameworks, multi-currency, schema) and just make it framework-led and country-name-free — the opposite of collapsing it.
  • The honest-scope pattern (“what it does — and what it won’t”). Keep everywhere.

Also flagged: 23 of 30 components are dead code (~5,200 LOC), ArticlePage still renders in the legacy orange theme, /assets + /404 use the old header/footer, and Instrument Serif never shipped (grass is Archivo-only).

2The new site architecture

Compliance vision leads. Two buyer doors underneath (clinics / hospitals). Worldwide, framework-differentiated, grass throughout. No country names.
New nav:   Platform · Frameworks · For Clinicians · For Hospitals · Pricing · Resources  +  Book a demo Start free

Platform = the agent + the connections + how it works (the vision). Frameworks = the differentiation grid (any standard, global). For Clinicians carries the clinical-relief wedge. The nav is the strategy.
