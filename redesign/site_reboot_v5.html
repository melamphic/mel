What Salvia will not do.

Worth writing down, because every other vendor in this market is careful not to.

NEVERUpcode. We will not suggest a diagnosis or a procedure that did not happen, at any margin.
NEVERWrite a clinical note on a clinician's behalf. We show what is missing and ask the person who knows.
NEVERSubmit anything without a person on your team approving it. Autopilot still has a pilot.
NEVERTake a percentage of your claim value. Our incentive should not move with your billing.
NEVERAsk your ward staff to work differently to make our software look good.
NEVERGive a false all clear. If the theatre note never arrived it says not received, never fine.

Show us one week of discharged files.

We will sit with your insurance desk for a day, take the last week of files you have already closed, and show you what was missing and the exact moment it could still have been produced. No slides.

Two systems on one page

Everything inside a product frame is Hairline, rendered from the real tokens. Everything outside it is the brand. They never mix, which is what stops the product shots looking like a different company's software.

Hairline, verbatimCobalt #1B44C0, ground #F7F6F3, Public Sans, 1px borders, no shadows. Radius 0 rows, 2 tags, 4 buttons, 6 panels. Straight from src/theme/tokens.ts.
Status is a dot and a word7px dot, never a container, never colour alone. Received. Not received. Queried. Exactly as the Status component ships.
Sentence case everywhere in the productThe earlier fragments shouted AWAITING DOCS and MISSING in caps. Hairline rule 8 forbids it. Fixed.
The five real claim statesAwaiting docs outlined, Submitted filled grey, Queried amber, Deducted red, Settled outlined. Shape carries the state as well as hue.
The real clockSix hour breach threshold, "5h 48m left", "overdue 3h 20m", turning bad red at 600 weight. Same logic as utils/clock.ts.
The chrome band#E7F0F6 behind every page title, bed badge as the lead, detail line joined with " · ". That header is the most recognisable thing in the app.
Still open · section orderThe narrative research is running. Fragments move wherever the architecture lands.
