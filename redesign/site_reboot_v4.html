You do not wait for an integration to start.

An HMS integration is a project with a timeline. The desk should not sit idle until it finishes, so it does not. You work from the first admission, and the integration deepens what you see rather than switching it on.

Nothing changes on the ward

Your clinical staff keep working exactly as they do today. That is a design constraint, not a nice to have.

Live from the start

You do not wait for the integration to finish before the desk is useful. It works from the first admission.

Built for the national rails

Our claim model follows NHCX's own workflow vocabulary, so the transport is a swap rather than a rebuild.

What Salvia will not do.

Worth writing down, because every other vendor in this market is careful not to.

NEVERUpcode. We will not suggest a diagnosis or a procedure that did not happen, at any margin.
NEVERWrite a clinical note on a clinician's behalf. We show what is missing and ask the person who knows.
NEVERSubmit anything without a person on your team approving it. Autopilot still has a pilot.
NEVERTake a percentage of your claim value. Our incentive should not move with your billing.
NEVERAsk your ward staff to work differently to make our software look good.
NEVERGive a false all clear. If the theatre note never arrived it says not received, never fine.

Show us one week of discharged files.

We will sit with your insurance desk for a day, take the last week of files you have already closed, and show you what was missing and the exact moment it could still have been produced. No slides.

Six playing fragments, one pause each

Hero grid · evidence arriving · the file checking itself · state progressing to settled · the claim file on the gradient · counting stats with a growing chart and a live worklist · the connection diagram. All HTML and CSS. No video, no gif, no Lottie.

Chrome holds still, data movesPanel borders, headers and labels never animate. Only the values inside them change, which is what makes it read as a product rather than a graphic.
Every block has its own pauseHover any fragment and the control appears. Same move the references make, and it signals the motion is worth controlling rather than hiding.
Real product strings onlyIP 2026/4471, Bed B-207, Star Health via Medi Assist, Awaiting docs, Queried, Settled. All lifted from the shipped app.
Nothing plays off screenCounters and the chart wait for the section to enter the viewport, then run once. The loops idle when scrolled past.
No shadows, no glowHairlines and flat fills. The gradient field appears exactly once, behind the single most important frame.
Reduced motion renders the last frameNot a degraded wobble. Already wired.
