03 PROBLEM
The problem

Three teams touch one admission.
None of them can see each other.

The ward writes the notes. The counselling desk handles the patient. The insurance department receives a finished pile of paper, scans it, uploads it, then argues about it weeks later.

By the time anyone notices a document is missing, the patient has gone home and the evidence can no longer be created. The bed stays blocked while the file gets assembled after the fact.

MOVIE 02 · THE GAP Three horizontal lanes, ward / counselling / insurance, each producing its own paper on its own clock. Documents slide right and pile up at a wall marked DISCHARGE. One is missing. The pile stops. Then the same three lanes replay on a single shared track and the pile clears. Roughly eight seconds, loops.

Rules that keep it out of AI slop territory

Motion equals a state change. Nothing floats, bobs, parallaxes or shimmers for decoration. If a thing moves, it is because that thing actually happens in the product.
Real strings, never lorem. Bed 214. OT note. Pre-auth 4/6. Implant sticker missing. A hospital person should recognise every word on screen.
One thing moves at a time. Inside a single frame, one change, then rest. Three simultaneous animations is what makes a page feel generated.
No bounce, no spring, no glow. cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), 240 to 400ms. Opacity and transform only. No drop shadows that pulse.
Green is a field, not a coat of paint. Mint appears as the ground behind a floating UI shot, and as the one primary button. Body text and borders stay neutral.
Pausable and reduced-motion honest. Every loop has a pause control. prefers-reduced-motion renders the final frame, not a degraded wobble.
