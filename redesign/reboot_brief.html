0Where we are — don't restart, evolve

A strong plan exists. This brief adds the missing angles, the inspiration, and the sequencing.
The diagnosis and skeleton are done. The audit proved the confusion is one root cause — a beautiful India-first front-end over a multi-country / per-seat / four-vertical back-end, with the agentic-compliance north star absent. The overhaul plan already commits to: compliance-lead hero (Connect · Agent · Any framework), a new nav that is the strategy (Platform · Frameworks · For Clinicians · For Hospitals · Pricing · Resources), worldwide + framework-differentiated + no country names, and grass kept. We build on that — we don't redo it.

What your latest direction adds: make continuous compliance measurable the emotional core, make the real-time watch + alert-the-owner tangible, and make integrations a first-class promise — then purge every flag and India-residue so the global story is clean everywhere.

1The narrative arc — the Vanta playbook, completed for clinical

Four verbs. The current plan nails "connect" and "prove"; your direction completes "monitor" and "alert".
1 · Connect
Your clinical stack
Voice notes, consent, prescriptions, drug-admin, incidents, vitals — and your HMIS / EMR / LIS / PACS. Each becomes structured, checkable evidence.
2 · Measure
Make policy a number
Every policy that was a PDF nobody could prove is now scored on every note. The unmeasurable becomes a live metric.
3 · Monitor
Always on, real-time
A live posture score that moves as care happens — and the agent tells the person in charge the moment something drifts, not once a year.
4 · Prove — any framework, global
One capture engine → NABH · JCI · NSQHS · CMS · HEDIS · …
The agent maps your evidence to whatever standard you answer to, worldwide. Add a framework, not a rebuild. You confirm; it never submits unsupervised. The framework is the geography — the site never names a country.

This is the whole moat sentence in motion: "prove any framework from the clinical record." Nobody in scribe-land or GRC-land says it.

2The three sharpened angles (with real copy)

These are the new first-class stories. Each gets a homepage section and a page anchor.

A · Measure the unmeasurable new hero section

The single most differentiated line available. A policy has always been a document you hoped was followed. Salvia turns each into a measurement. This reframes the whole category from "documentation" to "measurement of care."

Section copy — candidate
The policy you couldn't measure is now a number.
You wrote the hand-hygiene rule, the consent SOP, the sepsis pathway. Could you ever prove they were followed — without a manual chart pull? Salvia scores every record against every policy the moment it's signed. A binder becomes a live gauge.

Visual: a policy → live % gauge. Left, the clause ("Hand hygiene documented before procedure"); right, a green number ticking with each note. Borrow the observability trope — "you can't improve what you can't measure" — applied to care, not servers.

B · Real-time, to the person in charge new section

"Always-on" only lands when it's tangible: a posture that updates as care happens, and a named human who is told the instant it slips. This is Vanta's "continuous, real-time evidence of your active controls — beyond a one-time snapshot," made clinical.

Section copy — candidate
Your quality lead finds out in real time — not at the audit.
Salvia watches every record as it's signed, keeps a live compliance posture, and flags the drift the moment it happens — the missing consent, the unmonitored high-risk drug, the pathway skipped. No more pre-assessment scramble.

Visual: the one-time-snapshot vs continuous contrast (a dusty binder / annual audit spike, versus a flat live line + an alert card to the compliance officer). Personify the agent with the real Copilot screenshots we already have (the propose→commit card is a perfect "you confirm; it never acts alone" proof).

C · Integrations — connect your stack promote to first-class

The plan has "Connections"; make it a proper section. Salvia sits on top of what a hospital already runs — it's additive, not rip-and-replace. This de-risks the buy and reinforces "the compliance layer over your systems."

Section copy — candidate
Works with what you already run.
Connect your HMIS, EMR, LIS and PACS — plus Salvia's own capture. Every source becomes structured evidence the agent reads. FHIR / HL7 in; audit-ready records out.

Visual: a connections grid (system logos / typed source tiles) flowing into one evidence graph (Vanta "connect your stack" analog; Zapier/Merge-style grid). Even pre-integrations, show the data-point tiles we do ingest — the surface area reads real.

6Art-direction recommendations (inside grass)

Five calls that keep the differentiator (clean/green/human) while raising the craft.
  • The product is the hero. Lead sections with real drawn Salvia UI (Copilot, coverage grid, policy-check rows, PDF hash) — Stripe/Retool ethos. We already have these; use them, not stock art.
  • One signature motion moment: the live posture gauge ticking up in green on the homepage. Everything else stays still — restraint (Linear) beats scattered animation.
  • The framework grid is the centerpiece of the differentiation — make it genuinely clickable and fed by the real coverage engine, not a static logo wall.
  • Personify the agent with the real thing — actual Copilot screenshots + a propose→commit card — never an abstract "AI" glow/blob.
  • Hold the discipline: white/cream · one green #48CD5F · Archivo · hairlines · generous whitespace. Borrow Linear's rhythm + Watershed's "green = serious measurement" credibility. Never navy, never purple-gradient, never a dark compliance skin (already rejected).

7The flags & residue to purge (global, everywhere)

The confusion is one root cause: an India-front over a multi-country back-end. Kill the axis, not just the veneer.
  • Country as an axis → gone. Remove CountrySwitcher.tsx, the 7-country signup dropdown, and the country filter on /frameworks. Frameworks are the geography.
  • Structured data leak. prerender.mjs bakes "Made in India 🇮🇳" + areaServed: AU, NZ, UK, IE, US + foreign-regulator copy into every page. Rewrite worldwide, framework-described, country-name-free.
  • Scribe framing. Demote ScribeVsSalvia.tsx and any "documentation software" lead — comparing to scribes keeps us in their category.
  • IA bloat. Fold 4 vertical + 6 discipline pages into For Clinicians; retire the off-strategy vet page; kill the dead blog rails (they 404).
  • Pricing = one source of truth. Collapse the three contradictory models in data/pricing.ts; keep multi-currency worldwide; reconcile on-page vs meta prices.
  • Fix-now bugs. /hospitals bakes as a 404 (SSR route table); ArticlePage on legacy orange; /assets + /404 on legacy header/footer; #demo anchor missing; ~23 dead components (~5,200 LOC).

Un-break /hospitals, one pricing source of truth, strip country/flags from structured data + signup, migrate the two legacy pages, delete dead code. No positioning decision needed — do first.
